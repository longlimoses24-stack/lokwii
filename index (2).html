About Us

Started by one woman who couldn't look away.

Hannah Foundation began with a simple, stubborn idea: that a child's future shouldn't be decided by what their parents can or can't afford. In Lorengchora, Napak District, many Karamojong families want school and stability for their children but are held back by poverty alone. Hannah Longoli founded the Foundation to close that gap — one child, one family at a time.

Today, a small team of five works directly in the community — identifying children in need, coordinating school enrollment, running skills training, and making sure no child in our care goes without shelter or food.

"We are not just paying school fees. We are telling a child that someone believes their life matters." — Hannah Longoli, Founder & Director
What We Provide

Six ways we show up for a child.

Shelter

Safe, stable housing for children whose home situations can't provide the security every child needs to grow.

Food & Nutrition

Regular meals so children can focus on learning and growing, not going hungry.

Education

School fees, scholastic materials, and enrollment support so no child is left out of the classroom for lack of funds.

Computer Skills

Basic computer literacy to prepare older children for modern work.

Farming & Animal Rearing

Practical agricultural and livestock skills rooted in Karamojong tradition and self-reliance.

Spiritual Growth

Guidance and community that nurtures each child's character and faith, not just their circumstances.
